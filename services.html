FDA QMSR / 21 CFR Part 820 deliverables

Structured, risk-based support for applicable quality-system and design-control documentation. Scope is tailored to the product, intended use, and regulatory pathway.

Project initiation & planning

Project charter, Design and Development Plan, development milestones, team responsibilities, design reviews, and deliverable ownership.

Design inputs & outputs

User needs, measurable design inputs, acceptance criteria, design outputs, specifications, traceability matrices, and review records.

Verification & validation

Design V&V plans and protocols, objective evidence, test reports, usability engineering, formative and summative evaluations, and feasibility-study planning/documentation.

DHF remediation & traceability

Gap assessments, remediation plans, design history file organization, risk-to-requirement traceability, and evidence closure.

Manufacturing setup & transfer

Manufacturing process setup, work instructions, equipment and process qualification planning, acceptance criteria, DFM, and design-transfer readiness.

Project & people management

Integrated plans, schedules, risks, stakeholder alignment, cross-functional leadership, resource planning, metrics, and delivery follow-up.

Clinical feasibility & surgeon feedback

Experience includes traveling with Marketing teams to engage surgeons, demonstrate products, gather voice-of-customer and workflow feedback, and translate user needs into product redesign inputs. Activities can support feasibility and usability planning; clinical activities require the appropriate approvals and protocols.

Analytics & engineering software

Power BI dashboards to visualize device performance and track metrics; project and quality systems including Agile, eQMS, Adaptive, ETQ, and Windchill; CAD/CAE tools including Siemens NX (Unigraphics), SolidWorks, Pro/ENGINEER, ANSYS, and Fluent.

Each engagement is scoped to the product, target market, project stage, and available evidence. Regulatory outcomes are not guaranteed.
