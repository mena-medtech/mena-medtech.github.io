About Ahmad Ali Krayem

I bring 15 years of experience across medical device and pharmaceutical environments, with work spanning R&D, quality, manufacturing engineering, design controls, regulatory documentation, and post-market activities.

In addition to my technical background, I am a people manager and CEO-minded business leader. I have managed teams of 50+ people, coordinated cross-functional stakeholders, and focused on executing and delivering projects on time.

My experience also includes traveling with Marketing teams to recruit and engage surgeons for product demonstrations, collect voice-of-customer feedback, and translate clinical workflow needs into product redesign inputs. I use Power BI to build performance dashboards and track device and project metrics, supporting data-informed decisions.
