About Ahmad Ali Krayem

Ahmad is a manufacturing and mechanical engineer with a master's degree in biomedical engineering and experience spanning medical-device product development, manufacturing, commercialization, and supply chain. He has contributed across the product lifecycle, from design and verification through process validation and production.

His work includes medical devices for cardiovascular, fertility, surgical, imaging, and patient-monitoring applications. He has held engineering and project roles with Johnson & Johnson/Abiomed, CooperSurgical, Takeda, Integra, Philips, GE Healthcare, and 3M.
